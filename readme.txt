STREAKUP
Small steps. Big progress.
=========================================

Build good habits, one day at a time. Free, and no sign up.


TRACKER
1. Type a habit and press "Add habit".
2. When you have done it today, press "Check in".

TIMER
25 minute focus timer.
Start begins it, Stop pauses it, Reset goes back to 25:00.
When the time is up, take a 5 minute break.

TIPS
Press "New tip" to see the next habit tip.


GOOD TO KNOW
- Habits are saved in your browser, on this device only.
- Clearing your browser data deletes them.
- A checked habit does not reset the next day.
- Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
